In 1995, he moved to another city because he entered in University to study Eletrical Engineering. He discovered a deep passion for research and went on to earn his Master's and PhD by developing computer models designed to read human handwriting.

Working at University

He achieved his lifelong dream of becoming an university teacher in 2009 by joining the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte. He helped build a foundational undergraduate program for future engineers and taught massive classes of up to 150 students.

Leadership and Research

Over time, he took on big leadership roles, managing the academic records for over 30,000 students and helping the institution to make adaptations during the COVID-19 pandemic. He also continued his research, using computer software to predict when machines and engines might break down.
